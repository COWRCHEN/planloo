# User Journeys — Example Configurations

Real-world buyer profiles showing what they'd buy, what they'd pay, and how that compares to the old tier model.

---

## Profile 1 — Casual User (Personal Events)

**Who**: Someone organizing one or two social or family events per year.

**What they need**: A bit more event capacity, a guest list larger than 50, and basic email invites.

| Item | Units | Price/mo |
|------|-------|----------|
| Events | 2 × $3 | $6.00 |
| Guests | 2 × $2 (200 guests) | $4.00 |
| Emails | 1 × $2 (1,000 emails) | $2.00 |
| **Total** | | **$12.00/mo** |

Old Personal tier: $19.99/mo. **Saves $7.99/mo (40%).**

CSV, budget tracking, task templates, and vendor management are included free.

## Cost Comparison Summary

| Profile | New Price | Old Closest Tier | Delta |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| Casual user | $12.00 | Personal $19.99 | **-$7.99 (-40%)** |
| Solo wedding planner | $44.00 | Planner $39.99 | +$4.01 (+10%) |
| Corporate event manager | $45.00 | Planner $39.99 | +$5.01 (+13%) |
| Small agency | $197.00 | Agency $199.99 | **-$2.99 (-1.5%)** |
| Minimalist | $15.00 | Personal $19.99 | **-$4.99 (-25%)** |

Light users save the most. Power users who were maxing out old tier limits pay a small premium but gain elasticity — they can scale up or down each month.
